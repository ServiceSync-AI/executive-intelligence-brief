GM Ordering Process

I. Pull Parts Requests
•

Throughout the day, all C O and OV parts that need to be ordered for service and
customers.

2. Submit Daily GM Order
•

Submit all CSO and OVN parts to GM every day at 1:50 PM.

3. Review Order Results
•

After submission, review the order to see what filled and what went on backorder.

4. Open SPAC Case
•

Any part that goes on backorder gets a SPAC case opened immediately.

5. Update Tekion
•

Enter the SPAC ca e number in the comments section in Tekion so everyone can see
the status.

6. Communicate with Service Advisors
•

Notify the service advisor if their part is on backorder and let them know a SPAC case
has been submitted.

7. Receive and Check In Parts
•

As parts arrive, receive them in the ystem and verify they're correct and accounted for.

8. Notify When Parts Arrive
•
•

For service orders, notify the service advisor that the parts are here.
For over-the-counter customers, contact the customer to let them know their part has
arrived.

9. Weekly Unbilled Parts Report (Every Wednesday)
•

Send an email to the Fixed Ops Director and Service Director listing parts that have
been received for the service department but not yet billed out.
