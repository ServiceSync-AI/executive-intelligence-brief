Technician Hour Accountability combined with Menu-Driven Selling. Service operations are managed as a production
environment with daily and monthly targets for available versus billed hours. Advisors sell from a structured service
menu to drive consistency, increase effective labor rate, and improve hours per RO.
Why It Works:
•

Improves billed hours and labor gross

•

Creates advisor consistency regardless of staffing

•

Reduces unpaid diagnostic time and inefficiencies

•

Exposes opportunities early instead of at month-end

3. Service Menu Usage
Advisors sell from a structured service menu rather than memory. Menus clearly separate maintenance and repair
recommendations to support consistent pricing, improve customer understanding, and reduce discretionary discounting.

4. Special Order Parts (SOP) Process

•

Advisor adds required special-order parts to the Repair Order (RO).
Parts department orders all parts listed on the RO.

•

A weekly Special Order Parts report is printed and reviewed .

•

Once all parts for a customer vehicle have arrived, the report is provided to BOC.

•

BOC contacts the customer and schedules the appointment only when all parts are in stock.

This process reduces aging special-order inventory, protects cash flow, improves technician efficiency, and ensures
repairs are completed in one visit whenever possible.
