Service Advisor Best Practices Checklist

Dealership Name _____________________________________________________       Group __ __ __ __ - __ __

Before the service visit
 X or  = Yes


	•	Do you have someone other than the service advisor making service appointments?



	•	Are service histories of appointments pulled ahead of arrival and reviewed?



	•	Do you pre-write the RO (with suggestions) before the customer arrives?



	•	Do you post appointment customer names in the service write-up area for all to see?



Once the customer arrives



	•	When a customer arrives, is someone greeting them immediately?



	•	Does the person greeting them take down their name, mileage and VIN?



	•	Does the person greeting them acknowledge their appointment?



	•	When the service advisor meets with the customer, do they have a RO and service history?



	•	Does the service advisor walk around the customers’ vehicle with them almost 100% of the time?



	•	Do your service advisor’s use electronic tablets during the walk-around and sales process?



	•	During the walk-around, do service advisors turn on the lights and check the bulbs?



	•	During the walk-around, do the service advisors check each of the tires?



	•	During the walk-around, do the service advisors or someone else raise the hood?



	•	With the hood raised during the service reception, does someone take fluid samples?



	•	During the walk-around, do the service advisors appraise the customer of tires, bulbs, fluids, etc.?



	•	Does the service advisor write the main item the customer is in for, as the first item on the RO?



	•	Does the service advisor review the service history with the customer?



	•	Does the service advisor give the customer a copy of the service menu during the write-up?



	•	Does the service advisor offer and ask permission to do a complimentary multi-point inspection?



	•	Does the service advisor recommend additional services or special or offer specials?



	•	Does the service advisor promise to follow up at a certain time with a status report?



	•	Does the service advisor collect work and cell phone numbers, and email addresses?



	•	Does the service advisor review the RO with the customer before asking them to sign it?



	•	Does the service advisor show the customer to the customer lounge, shuttle or rental car?



	•	Does the service advisors have a competitive pricing board in the write-up area?



	•	Do the service advisors have tires and wheels displayed in the write-up area?



	•	Do the service advisors have worn parts display in the write-up area?



	•	Do the service advisors have a marquee board with specials listed in the write-up area?




After the customer has left his or her vehicle



	•	Does service advisor attach a multi-point inspection (MPI) form to the RO, or is it electronic?



	•	When techs get RO and vehicle, is the MPI done right away and findings sent to service advisor?



	•	Do service advisors have a uniform pricing guide for quick estimates?



	•	Do techs, service advisors and parts have radios or phones to communicate with each other?



	•	Does the service advisor immediately communicate MPI findings to the customer?



	•	Do the service advisors give customers updates and status reports?



	•	Do your service advisors immediately close the RO, or approve it before it is closed?



	•	Do service advisors notify customers when the job is finished, explain and give the final cost?



	•	If the customer is not waiting, can the SERVICE ADVISOR email the completed RO to the customer?



	•	Do service advisors confirm customers’ pickup times and give them instructions for pickup?



When customers pick up their vehicle



	•	Do the service advisors see the customer when they come to pick up their vehicle?



	•	Do service advisors go over work done on the vehicle and review findings/recommendations?



	•	Do the service advisors cashier their own tickets?



	•	Do the service advisors schedule the next service appointment with the customer?



	•	Do the service advisors walk the customer to the cashier?



	•	Do the service advisors arrange for the vehicle delivery, or parked in a convenient space?



	•	Do the service advisors deliver the vehicle to the customer themselves?



	•	Do service advisors ensure customer vehicles are as clean as, or cleaner than when brought in?



After customers have left with their vehicle



	•	Does the service advisor, (or someone) follow up after every visit to be sure of satisfaction?



	•	Does manager or someone review every RO, every day, and go over with SERVICE ADVISOR?



	•	Do you furnish daily (or weekly) reports to service advisors on their ELR and FRH/RO?



	•	Do you track MPI additional service recommendation (ASR’s) averages, by tech?



	•	Do you track MPI ASR sales and closing ratio averages by SERVICE ADVISOR?



	•	Do service advisor’s (or BDC) follow up on all unsold additional recommended services (ASR’s) at a certain time after decliners leave?



	•	Do you post monthly ASR reports for techs and service advisors to see?



Give yourself two points for every “yes” answer, then add up your score.



How close to a score of 100 are you?  What is an “A” grade?  What is a “B”?



Are you a member of the honor society, or just passing?



Do you think your score has any correlation with your sales per RO, or your CSI?
