Parts special-order process

Any part not available off the shelf or locally will be ordered.
When the part Is available through the Denver PDC, it is ordered via a standard CSO.
If the part is not available from the Denver PDC, it will be placed on an OVN order if
available and the vehicle is remaining at the dealer or is down.
Once the ordered part arrives at the dealership and is received into inventory, service
advisors are notified, and each advisor receives a list of all CSO special-order parts twice
weekly.
Special-order parts will remain in the CSO bin until installed or for up to 45 days.
Any special-order part not installed within 45 days will be sold to another customer or
returned to GM.

Please note that all wholesale and customer-pay parts must be prepaid prior to ordering.
