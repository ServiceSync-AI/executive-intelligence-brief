How to share your Google Business Profile with NADA Analytics.
 
	•	Open a Google browser (Chrome)

	•	Make sure you are logged in under the profile that manages your dealership profile.



	•	Search your Business Name


	•	Your Business profile edit features will load



	•	
	•	Click on the 3 dots on the right of them



	•	Click on Business Profile settings



	•	Select People & Access



	•	
	•	When users pop up, select “Add”


	•	Enter the  Assigned email address you received from your consultant



	•	Then click on “Invite”



The request will go to the team at Vistadash and bee approved. 
Once approved, data will begin transferring and become visible in the following month composite.
